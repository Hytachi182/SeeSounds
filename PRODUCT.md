# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

Python 3.11+, PySide6, QtMultimedia, SQLite, RapidFuzz, pytest, PyInstaller.

## Users

People preparing locally for an exam that asks them to recognise audio clips.

## Product Purpose

Sound Recognition Trainer lets learners organise MP3 files, skip spoken introductions through a per-sound offset, practise safely, and measure exam readiness.

## Positioning

It turns a personal, offline MP3 collection into repeatable training and two examination modes while retaining each answer and its evidence locally.

## Operating Context

Desktop use on Windows and macOS, often alongside an existing folder of MP3 reference sounds. No cloud service is required.

## Capabilities and Constraints

All data is local SQLite. Original audio files are never edited. Playback begins at a configured offset. Free-text assessment tolerates casing, accents, punctuation, aliases, and minor typos.

## Evidence on Hand

The functional specification supplied by the requester. No sound assets or branding assets have been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make the next study action obvious.
- Preserve learner control and show the evidence behind a score.
- Keep setup and recovery understandable when files move.
- Remain lightweight and offline-first.
